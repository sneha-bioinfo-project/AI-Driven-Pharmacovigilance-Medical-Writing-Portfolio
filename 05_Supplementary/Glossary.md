# Medical & Technical Glossary

## Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR)

A harmful and unintended response to a medication occurring at doses normally used in humans for prevention, diagnosis, or treatment.

---

## Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)

A national digital health initiative in India designed to establish interoperable health information systems and digital healthcare infrastructure.

---

## Class Imbalance

A machine learning dataset condition where one class significantly outnumbers another. In ADR prediction, non-ADR events often greatly exceed true ADR cases, requiring approaches such as Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE).

---

## Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS)

A software-based system integrated with electronic health records (EHRs) that provides healthcare professionals with patient-specific clinical alerts and decision-support information.

---

## Electronic Health Record (EHR)

A digitised, longitudinal collection of patient health information, including clinical notes, laboratory results, diagnoses, medication records, and other healthcare data.

---

## Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)

Machine learning methods designed to improve transparency by explaining model predictions and identifying important contributing features.

---

## FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS)

A database maintained by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration containing spontaneous post-marketing adverse event and medication error reports used for pharmacovigilance activities.

---

## Federated Learning

A privacy-preserving machine learning approach that enables multiple institutions to collaboratively train models while keeping raw patient data within local systems.

---

## MedDRA

Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities, a standardised medical terminology used internationally for classification and reporting of adverse events.

---

## MIMIC-III

Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care III, an open-access database containing de-identified intensive care unit (ICU) patient data for clinical research and informatics studies.

---

## Pharmacovigilance

The science and activities related to the detection, assessment, understanding, and prevention of adverse effects or other medicine-related problems.

---

## RxNorm

A standardised clinical drug terminology developed by the U.S. National Library of Medicine that provides consistent names and identifiers for medications.

---

## SMOTE

Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique, a machine learning approach that generates synthetic samples from minority classes to reduce dataset imbalance.

---

## SIDER

Side Effect Resource, a curated database containing drug–side effect associations extracted from drug labels and related biomedical resources.
